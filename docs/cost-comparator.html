Canton design cost comparator

Compare what two designs of the same transaction cost in Canton traffic, before building either. Every term comes from bills read off a metered Canton 3.4.11 network, and each one says whether it was measured or is an extrapolation. Prices use the $60/MB list price of CIP-0129 (Universal Burn, approved 2026-10-23). Its volume curve and discounts are left to a second CIP; the ones here are the illustrative numbers from the CIP's deck.

Volume and pricing

Volume tiers are counted in MB, as in the CIP's deck (illustrative): your TPS is converted into "typical" 16.7 KB transactions before the tier is applied, so a smaller transaction also lands in a lower tier. The Global Synchronizer keeps one price at every volume.

How each number is derived
Bytes are what the sequencer billed on a local metered network (one sequencer, one mediator, up to eight participants, in memory); the estimate Canton returns at prepare time came within about 4% of them. MainNet transactions carry more (featured-app markers, rewards bookkeeping), so read absolute figures as a floor and differences between designs as the robust part.
