The gap widens with pressure Reservation buys 5% throughput at concurrency 8, but 15% at concurrency 32 — and at 32 it is also faster (p99 1902 → 1763 ms). There is no latency price for removing contention; the harder you push, the more selection strategy is worth.
A correction to an earlier number With a pool sized so depth stays roughly constant, single-wallet random selection contends 12.5% — not the 48.8% reported in the first scaling sweep. That run's pool drained 150 → 30, and a shrinking pool inflated the collision rate. Pool depth and selection strategy are separate effects, and the earlier figure confounded them.
